AI Usage

I am a developer who does use Artificial Intelligence (AI) - and I focus on using it responsibly. I own and take accountability for all the work I generate - and if an AI helps, then I give attribution to both of us.

I am perfectly capable of writing CR@P CODE MYSELF!

The AI helps me get places faster - and I am able to accelerate my learning along the way.

I use AI to do the following:

  • Help me learn and onboard with tools I am not familiar with (Jekyll, GitHub Pages)
  • Help me debug when I am tired of squeezing my rubber duck
  • Help me fast-track boilerplate tasks that I know I could do myself - and want done faster

The AI tools I have used so far:

  • GitHub Copilot (In the IDE)
  • ChatGPT (in the Browser)
  • Claude (as a dedicated Application)

I recognize:

  • Data provided in prompts to AI tools is likely to be used as training data
  • There is a good chance (despite promises being made against it) that data the AI interacts with during a prompt can be used as training data
  • AI is a really good next-word-predictor and as such, mimics what it has heard in the past without ever TRULY understanding
  • Passing off AI code as my own without attribution is deceitful, and I will not do that - hence the disclaimer button
