Obsidian has [callouts](https://obsidian.md/help/callouts) notation which are the same as Markdown Blockquotes. The supported callouts are: info, todo, tip/hint/important, success/check/done, question/help/faq, warning/caution/attention, failure/fail/missing, danger/error, bug, example, quote/site. This website should mirror the styling we see in Obsidian

> This is a note callout
