## AI Usage

I am a developer who does use Artificial Intelligence (AI) - and I focus on using it responsibly. I own and take
accountability for all the work I generate - and if an AI helps, then I give attribution to both of us.

> NOTE:
>
> I am perfectly capable of writing **CR@P CODE MYSELF**! I do not _require_ need an AI to write cr@p code on my behalf 😏
>
> The AI helps me get places faster ⚡, and I am able to speed up my learning along the way.

I use AI to do the following:

- Help me learn and onboard with tools I am not familiar with (Jekyll, GitHub Pages)
- Help me debug when I am tired of squeezing my rubber duck
- Help me fast-track boilerplate tasks that I know I could do myself - and want done faster

The AI tools I have used so far:

- GitHub Copilot (In the IDE)
- ChatGPT (in the Browser)
- Claude (as a dedicated Application)

I recognize:

- Data provided in prompts to AI tools is likely to be used as training data
- There is a good chance (despite promises being made against it) that data the AI interacts with during a prompt can be
  used as training data
- AI is a really good next-word-predictor and as such, mimics what it has heard in the past without ever *TRULY*
  understanding
- Passing off AI code as my own without attribution is deceitful, and I will not do that - hence the disclaimer button

Comments:

- If my code exists in the public domain, I cannot detect/stop AI being used on it
    - As such, I am not going to police AI on my site – I will instead focus on what I like and consider AI to help me
      when relevant
- If an AI tool has been trained on a super large dataset, it can help me detect conventions and patterns used by a
  majority. I can then follow up on those things when solving problems
- If AI disappears / becomes unavailable (or becomes **sh!tt!er**), I owe it to myself and my peers that I can operate
  without it; aware that it will be slower
