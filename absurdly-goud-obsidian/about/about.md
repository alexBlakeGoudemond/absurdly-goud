## Website Name

`ABsurdly Goud` is a personal website. Its name came from a few pieces of inspiration:

- My initials are ABG - I wanted that included in the name
- `absurd` means completely unreasonable, illogical, or lacking common sense
- `goud` is a reference to my surname (`Goudemond`) and reads as the word `good`
- Combining these two words together is fun and surprising - just the kind of thing I want my website to be
	- Together - could mean: `this is strange, but I like it` or any other interpretation you may have!
- A domain like `alexBlakeGoudemond.com` is then focussed on Identity and (more-so?) Professionalism
- It also reminds me to not be serious all the time 🧐😏
- All-in-all - I just like it, so it is `ABsurdlyGoud.com!` 💫

## Website Purpose?

I built this website for a combination of reasons that all blended together at the same time. In no particular order:
- I was exposed to the IndieWeb, and was captivated - I knew quickly that I wanted to join
- I have had some ideas for fun personal projects that I want to roll out - and this website has a very low barrier of entry for me to try those as Proof Of Concepts (POCs)
- I felt empowered to express myself freely and detach from Social Media a bit more
- I like the idea of revising my frontend skills; I spend much time on the backend at work

There is a kind of self-reflection on how I am building the site - via posts and notes. And by placing those ON the site - it feels like the website is documenting its own journey as I build it up piece by piece 🥳 

In few words, it exists:
- because it can 💪
- because building it and having it is fun 🎉
- because I want it to 🥰
- as an opportunity to grow and learn, striving to get better each day 📎

## Why IndieWeb?

A friend asked me: "Why build a website for the IndieWeb and not just a normal website?" and the question held me. We had discussed the obvious things before: 

- no priority on advertisements
- no need to chase algorithms
- no need to build around Search Engine Optimization (SEO) and trends
- no need to justify the website's existence

And upon reflection, I realized that these responses did not address the core of question...

Then my answer came to me: 

> "I want to be a part of the community. I want to rebel against the current Internet structure. I want to be in control of my own data, time, attention and engagement. I want to have fun, explore and grow"

I felt satisfied with that answer! And as an outcome: we will see if my friend joins the web as well! 😏

#### Opinion Pieces
I recognize:

- Data provided in prompts to AI tools is likely to be used as training data 📈
- There is a good chance that data the AI interacts with during a task can be used as training data
	- I am referring to data not included in the prompt - data in the codebase / file system
	- The AI companies promise the AI tools do not do this - and I am sceptical
- AI is a really good next-word-predictor 
	- It mimics what it has been exposed to in the past without ever *TRULY* understanding what it is saying. Hence *hallucinations* 🫨
- Passing off AI code as my own without attribution is deceitful, and I will not do that - hence the disclaimer button 

Comments:

- I am sceptical around AI companies promising that they will NOT collect data outside of the prompts
	- I am awaiting the day when we are told: "We have been scraping data for years - now buy this product..." ⚠️
	- To cope with this, I have a personal policy: Only use AI on code that is publicly available
		- This way - anyone **COULD** use AI on it, by cloning the repo ![[free-real-estate.gif]]
